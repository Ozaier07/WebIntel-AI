About WebIntel AI

AI-Powered Web Scraper & Intelligent Data Analytics Platform - Department of CSE, IET Lucknow, 2026-27

Problem

Useful information is spread across many sites. Manual collection is slow and inconsistent, and basic scrapers collect text without explaining it. WebIntel AI joins extraction, cleaning, AI analysis, history and export in one workspace.

Workflow

01 Configure
Enter URL, choose fields
02 Validate & fetch
SSRF check, robots.txt, timeouts
03 Parse & clean
Extract, normalise, deduplicate
04 AI analysis
Summary, keywords, category
05 Save & export
History, CSV / JSON

Technology

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend: Python, FastAPI
  • Scraping: Requests, BeautifulSoup
  • Data: SQLite, Pandas

Security & responsible scraping

  • SSRF protection, redirects re-checked
  • robots.txt respected
  • Timeouts and size limits
  • No bypassing logins, CAPTCHAs or paywalls

Roadmap

  • Done: foundation, scraping, analysis, history, export
  • Next: Anakin AI API, scheduling, change detection, analytics charts
